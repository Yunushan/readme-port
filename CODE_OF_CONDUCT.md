# Community Code of Conduct

ReadmePort welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participation requires respectful, constructive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and clarity.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and boundaries.
- Make room for newcomers to ask basic questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban accounts when needed to keep the community safe.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and project-run community spaces.
